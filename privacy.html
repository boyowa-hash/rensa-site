Privacy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

Rensa is a brand of Praxis Brands Ltd, trading as Stennah & Hope, registered in England and Wales (company number 17222158), at Unit 9, The Workstack, 599 Woolwich Road, Charlton, London SE7 7GS. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

What we collect

When you join the Rensa list we collect your email address and the products you told us you are interested in (candles, the ceramic, skin). We collect nothing else through this site.

Why, and on what basis

We use your email address only to tell you about Rensa launches, starting with the candles. We do this because you asked us to: our lawful basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who handles it

This website is hosted by GitHub. When you join the list, your details are saved to a private Google Sheet in our Google account; Google processes them on our behalf and may store them outside the UK under appropriate safeguards. When we send launch news we may use a specialist email service to deliver it; any such service acts only on our instructions. We never sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or until Rensa stops sending launch news, whichever comes first. If you unsubscribe, we remove you from our sending list.

Your rights

  • Unsubscribe from any email using the link at the bottom of it.
  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them.

For anything about your data, email hello@rensa.uk.
